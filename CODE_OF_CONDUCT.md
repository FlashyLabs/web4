# Code of conduct

Everyone who reads, corrects or discusses this map is expected to do so with the same care the map asks of itself: say what you measured, say how you know, and treat the people on the other side of an issue as colleagues.

## Expected

- Correct the map with evidence, and accept a correction to your correction the same way.
- Disagree about what is true; do not disagree about who somebody is.
- Assume a wrong status is stale rather than dishonest, and say so if you find it is the second.
- Keep private things private: a repo marked `private` on the map is not a target for guessing at its contents in the open.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind, in issues, pull requests, commits or anywhere the repository's name appears.
- Publishing somebody else's private information — contact details, credentials, internal hostnames — whether or not it looks harmless.
- Inventing a figure or a status and presenting it as measured.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that breaks these rules and may decline further contributions from anybody who breaks them repeatedly. Reports go to the contact in `SECURITY.md`, with the same caveat that file carries about the address being confirmed; a report about a maintainer goes to the accountable contact named in the estate's mesh handshake instead.

## Scope

This applies in every space this repository owns — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit history — and to anybody representing it elsewhere.
